# Premium Reporting Workflows (10 examples)

1. **Executive Summary** – One‑page PDF/markdown with quality score, top issues, and recommendations.
2. **Data Quality Dashboard** – JSON with time‑series of quality scores for automated monitoring.
3. **Compliance Report** – Checklist of GDPR/CCPA fields and their sanitization status.
4. **Duplicate Analysis** – Heatmap of duplicate columns and sample duplicate rows.
5. **PII Exposure Report** – List of detected PII types, counts, and masked examples per column.
6. **Secret Exposure Report** – Summary of found API keys, tokens, and private keys with risk levels.
7. **Before/After Comparison** – Side‑by‑side statistics showing impact of cleaning/sanitization.
8. **Validation Failure Report** – Detailed list of rows that failed each validation rule.
9. **Schema Drift Report** – Comparison of two datasets highlighting added/removed/changed columns.
10. **Automation‑Ready Report** – Machine‑readable JSON that can trigger alerts in a CI/CD pipeline.
