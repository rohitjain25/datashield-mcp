# Premium Dataset Comparison Workflows (10 examples)

1. **Customer Master vs. Marketing List** – Find leads in marketing not in master; detect updates to existing customers.
2. **Weekly Sales Snapshot** – Compare this week’s sales to last week’s: new orders, changed quantities, cancelled orders.
3. **Product Catalog Versions** – Detect new SKUs, discontinued items, price changes >10%.
4. **User Account Export** – Identify new accounts, deleted accounts, changed email or password flags.
5. **Clinical Trial Visits** – Match visit IDs across waves; flag missing or extra visits.
6. **Inventory Levels** – Compare current stock to previous count; highlight discrepancies and shrinkage.
7. **Website Traffic Logs** – Compare hourly traffic; detect spikes, drops, and new referral sources.
8. **Email Campaign Metrics** – Compare open/click/bounce rates between A/B test groups.
9. **Financial Statements** – Compare balance sheet items across quarters; highlight variances.
10. **Software Release Assets** – Compare file manifests; detect new, missing, or changed binaries/checksums.
