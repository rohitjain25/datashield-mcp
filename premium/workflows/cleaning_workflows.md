15. **Event Registrations** – Normalize ticket types, validate email domains, detect duplicate registrations.

18. **Logistics Invoice** – Validate tax IDs, detect duplicate invoice numbers, validate line‑item totals.

22. **Product Catalog** – Normalize SKUs, validate price fields, detect duplicate product names.
